Common questions

What do I need to register a Private Limited company?

At least two directors (one must be resident in India) and at least two shareholders, plus identity and address proof for each, and proof of the registered office address.

When is GST registration required?

Generally when annual turnover crosses ₹40 lakh for most suppliers of goods or ₹20 lakh for service providers, with lower limits in some states. Some businesses, such as those making inter-state supplies of goods or selling goods through e-commerce platforms, may need to register regardless of turnover. Service providers making inter-state supplies generally don't need to register until they cross the threshold.

Is MSME (Udyam) registration free?

Yes. Udyam registration on the government portal is free. It can help with access to certain schemes, credit benefits and protection against delayed payments.

Why do co-founders need an agreement?

It records who owns what, roles, vesting, decision-making and what happens if someone leaves. These are the most common sources of disputes between co-founders.
