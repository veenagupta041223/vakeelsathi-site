Common questions

Where do I file a consumer complaint?

It depends on the amount you paid for the goods or services, not the compensation you claim. If you paid up to ₹50 lakh, the complaint goes to the District Consumer Commission; above ₹50 lakh and up to ₹2 crore, to the State Commission; and above ₹2 crore, to the National Commission. Complaints can also be filed online through the government's consumer e-filing portal.

Is there a deadline for consumer complaints?

Generally within two years from when the problem arose, under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

How soon must an accident compensation claim be filed?

The Motor Vehicles Act, as amended in 2019, requires a claim before the Motor Accident Claims Tribunal (MACT) to be filed within six months of the accident, so it's important not to delay.

Can I contest an e-challan?

Yes. Depending on your state, e-challans can be contested through the traffic police grievance process, the virtual court, or a Lok Adalat.
