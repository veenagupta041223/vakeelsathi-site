  • To prevent fraud and ensure website security

4. How We Use Your Data

Your personal data is used to:

  • Provide and improve our legal services
  • Communicate with you about service updates
  • Send you promotional materials (with your consent)
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Analyze usage patterns and improve website functionality
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share your name, phone number, or the details of your legal issue with advertising providers. However, we may share data with:

  • Service Providers: Hosting and infrastructure providers, including cloud storage services that receive, process, and store intake-form submissions on our behalf, and email service providers (all on a need-to-know basis)
  • Analytics and Advertising Providers: PostHog (website analytics) and Google (Google Ads conversion measurement) receive device and usage data, such as pages visited, ad clicks, and whether an enquiry form was submitted, through cookies and similar technologies described in Section 10
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or court order
  • Business Partners: Only with your explicit consent

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Once these purposes no longer apply, we will delete or anonymize your personal data, except where retention is required by applicable law.

7. Your Data Protection Rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Obtain confirmation of whether we process your data
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data (right to be forgotten)
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured format
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for processing at any time
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request limitation of data processing

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@vakeelsathi.in with "Data Rights Request" in the subject line.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Secure access controls and authentication
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Employee training on data protection

9. Data Processing Agreements

When VakeelSathi engages third-party data processors (such as lawyers, service providers, or contractors), we ensure that:

  • Written Data Processing Agreements (DPA) are in place
  • Processors only process data as per our instructions
  • Processors implement adequate security measures
  • Sub-processors are disclosed and authorized

  • Data is processed only for specified, lawful purposes

You have the right to request details of our data processors by contacting support@vakeelsathi.in.
